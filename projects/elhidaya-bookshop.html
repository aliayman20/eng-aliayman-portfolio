Project overview

Elhidaya Bookshop brings book discovery and shopping into a responsive online store. The project combines catalogue browsing and best-seller sections with cart functionality and bilingual support.

My role

I developed a responsive online bookshop with WordPress, Elementor, and WooCommerce, including product organization, shopping features, and bilingual support.

Main features & work completed

  • Book categories for browsing the catalogue.
  • Best-seller sections to highlight titles.
  • Cart functionality through WooCommerce.
  • Bilingual support and responsive layouts across devices.

Technical details

The website uses WordPress with Elementor and WooCommerce. The project combines page design and e-commerce functionality within this established platform.

Results & outcome

The completed online bookshop brings book discovery, best-seller browsing, and cart functionality into a bilingual shopping experience.
