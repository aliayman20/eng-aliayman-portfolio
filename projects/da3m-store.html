Project overview

DA3M Store presents IT infrastructure and networking products through an online storefront. Its purpose is to support product browsing and shopping, with categories, cart, wishlist, and account functionality.

My role

I developed the IT e-commerce website using WordPress, Elementor, and WooCommerce, with a product-focused interface for infrastructure and networking equipment.

Main features & work completed

  • Product categories for IT infrastructure and networking products.
  • Shopping cart functionality.
  • Wishlist and customer account features.
  • A responsive storefront focused on browsing products.

Technical details

WordPress provides the site platform, Elementor supports the page layouts, and WooCommerce provides the e-commerce layer.

Results & outcome

The result is an IT e-commerce storefront that combines product discovery with cart, wishlist, and account functionality.
