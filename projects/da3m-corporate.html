Project overview

DA3M needed a corporate web presence for its integrated IT solutions. The project brings company services, client work, careers, and contact information into a consistent technology-focused interface.

My role

I developed the responsive corporate website and its frontend presentation for an integrated IT solutions company, using HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript.

Main features & work completed

  • A technology-focused corporate visual identity.
  • Service, client, and project sections to present the company's work.
  • Careers, blog, and contact sections.
  • Responsive page layouts for desktop and mobile visitors.

Results & outcome

The completed website presents the company's IT services and project information in a responsive corporate interface, with clear routes to contact and company content.
